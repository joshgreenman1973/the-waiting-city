# Problems inside the sources themselves (recorded, not corrected)

Found by three blind-check passes on Sept. 5, 2026. These are defects in the agencies' own publications. The registry prints them next to the affected figures rather than smoothing them over.

- Mayor's Management Report dataset (rbed-zzin) never states that "(hours:minutes)" and "(minutes:seconds)" indicators are stored as H.MM and M.SS; the encoding is inferred from the fractional part never exceeding .59.
- NYCHA heat outage time: fiscal-year-to-date value covers heating months only; July to September rows are 0.00.
- HPD "Section 8 — Median time from completed application to voucher issuance" and "... from voucher issuance to lease up": titles say median, definitions say average; the first also includes incomplete applications.
- HPD "Lottery projects — Median time to complete applicant approvals": definition text garbled ("to approval to completion"). HPD "voluntary new construction unit" lease-up definition has typos ("revealed TCO", "HPS").
- HPD "Lottery units — Median time to approve an applicant": clock starts at certificate of occupancy, not at the applicant's log number, which is how the press and the Furman Center describe the figure.
- HRA "Average wait time to speak with a customer service agent": an in-person greeter-to-caseworker wait, not a phone wait. The printed PMMR shows "90:60" for a minutes:seconds value.
- HRA SNAP timeliness: November 2025 row blank. HRA Medicaid timeliness: no values after fiscal 2024.
- Commission on Human Rights "Open matters": "matters" undefined.
- Quarterly indicators carry valuedates that do not say which quarter a row closes; some rows repeat the prior month's value.
- NYCHA's Aug. 1, 2024 Section 8 release calls the same 200,000 "households," "applications," "New Yorkers" and "families and individuals."
- Comptroller's "Evictions Up, Representation Down" (May 2025) gives three framings for one rise in active eviction cases: from the January 2021 end of the moratorium (introduction), a moratorium that "expired on January 15, 2022" (body), and March 2020 to March 2024 (key findings).
- Furman Center's Housing Connect report does not state the date range of its sample.
- Gothamist's Dec. 18, 2025 vendor story says "licenses" where the DOHMH rule governs "permits."
- NYCHA Metrics repair-time page carries no as-of date.
